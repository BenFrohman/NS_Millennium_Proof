# Status snapshot — 13 October 2026

Current state of the Frohmanian Symplectic Tether formalization (Lean 4).

## Claimed result (proved conditionally)

The conditional form of Clay’s Navier–Stokes global-regularity statement is proved.

`frohmanian_tether_theorem` is the implication from the two inputs

- `hKato` — a short-time Kato solution from smooth, divergence-free, finite-energy data,
- `hDI` — the differential inequality \(M' \le C_{CZ} M^2 - \kappa'' M^3\),

together with the tether axioms, to a `TetherKernel` plus a velocity–pressure pair that satisfies the exact classical NS_PDE, matches the initial field, stays smooth for every \(t \ge 0\), has nonnegative vorticity supremum, and remains divergence-free.

Given the rate (`hDI`), the Riccati lemma yields the ceiling \(\max(M(0), C_{CZ}/\kappa'')\).
Given that ceiling, Kato local existence, a restart length, and uniqueness, the restart argument produces the global smooth solution.
The tether classification is closed: any bracket with the stated properties equals `TetherKernel`.

Both `frohmanian_tether_theorem` and the companion global-regularity statements are free of `sorryAx` under these hypotheses. The assembly is the proved implication.

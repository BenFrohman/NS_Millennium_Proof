                                                Abstract
          We record the classical Beale–Kato–Majda continuation criterion in self-contained form.
      For a maximal Kato solution of the 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes equations on T3 , inte-
      grability of the vorticity supremum in time implies that the solution extends globally and is
      smooth for positive times. (The classical criterion is stated here in the one-directional con-
      tinuation form that the argument proves.) The argument uses only the logarithmic Sobolev
      inequality, Kato–Ponce commutators, a log-Gronwall estimate, and standard parabolic boot-
      strap; it contains no circularity and no auxiliary fields with incorrect signs.

free. Let u ∈ C([0, T ∗ ); H s (T3 )) be the unique maximal local solution of the 3D incompressible
Navier–Stokes equations on T3 . If

operator is not bounded on L∞ . The sharp substitute is the logarithmic estimate (Beale–Kato–
Majda / Kozono–Taniuchi): for s > 5/2,

to ∥ω∥H s .)

2.4   Continuation and parabolic bootstrap
Kato’s local existence theorem asserts that a state with ∥u∥H s ≤ K may be continued for a
positive time of order 1/K. Because the H s norm remains bounded up to T ∗ , the solution can
be extended past any finite T ∗ , a contradiction unless T ∗ = +∞.

Problem
Benjamin Frohman
Independent Researcher

Abstract
The Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem remains one of the six unsolved
Clay Millennium Prize Problems as of April 2026. It asks whether, for the 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes equations with positive viscosity ν > 0, any smooth, divergence-free
initial velocity field u0 (x) on R3 or on the periodic torus produces a globally smooth solution u(x, t) and pressure p(x, t) for all t > 0, or whether a singularity can form in finite
time while the kinetic energy remains bounded. In this note I present two novel, dualtestable conjectures—the Frohman Symplectic Regularization Conjecture and the Frohman
Holographic Dual Regularization Conjecture—that offer concrete pathways to resolve this
question. These proposals integrate geometric hydrodynamics, artificial intelligence, fluidgravity duality, and laboratory measurements in ways not previously synthesized. I detail
their mathematical foundations, proposed testing protocols, falsifiability criteria, and potential implications. The conjectures are offered openly to the scientific community with an
invitation for rigorous testing and collaboration. Preliminary small-scale simulations and
visualizations are discussed as starting points accessible to independent researchers.

Introduction

Turbulence is the default behavior of real fluids at suﬀiciently high Reynolds numbers. It
appears in air flowing over wings, blood in vessels, atmospheric weather, ocean currents, engine
combustion, and industrial pipe flows. The incompressible Navier–Stokes equations govern all
these phenomena, yet in three dimensions the fundamental question of global regularity versus
finite-time singularity formation remains open.
A high-fidelity numerical simulation of turbulent flow—showing vorticity magnitude or enstrophy in pseudo-color across three stacked panels—vividly captures the stakes. This “milliondollar storm” illustrates why the problem matters: we can write the equations, but we do not
know whether they remain under mathematical control for all smooth initial data.
Traditional approaches have achieved important advances but have not settled the global
3D case. Here I propose two outside-the-box yet grounded conjectures designed to overcome
these barriers.

2

The Frohman Symplectic Regularization Conjecture

The Frohman Symplectic Regularization Conjecture posits that the Navier–Stokes equations,
viewed on the infinite-dimensional manifold of divergence-free vector fields, admit an undiscovered symplectic or multisymplectic structure. This hidden structure would enforce global
bounds on higher norms and regularize the nonlinear vortex-stretching term ω · ∇u.

The Frohman Holographic Dual Regularization Conjecture

The Frohman Holographic Dual Regularization Conjecture asserts an embedding into higherdimensional gravitational theory via fluid-gravity correspondence. Apparent singularities in the
fluid map to well-behaved bulk horizons protected by cosmic censorship.
This conjecture is particularly laboratory-accessible via quantum-fluid analogs and infrared
imaging.

Broader Implications, Community Call, and Next Steps

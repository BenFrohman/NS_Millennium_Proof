The Frohmanian Honest-Fill & Cryptographic Audit Solution (titan-audit)

To close the Frankenstein Proof loophole, a formal verification harness must enforce three separation
invariants simultaneously:
1. Immutable Specification Hash Pinning: The SHA-256 digest of all theorem signatures
and structure definitions is pinned externally; any agent edit to a theorem head (rather than
its proof body ‘:= by ...‘) triggers an immediate hard fail.
2. Metaprogramming Kernel Axiom Walk (Lean.collectAxioms): Every ‘TheoremInfo‘
in the compiled environment is inspected in-process to verify that its transitive axiom set is
a subset of {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}.
3. Vacuity Heuristics & Non-Vacuous Witness Gate: Automated AST scanning rejects ‘Prop := True‘ definitions, ‘(h : True)‘ binders, ‘False‘ hypotheses, and unguarded
real ‘iSup‘/‘sSup‘, and requires an explicit non-vacuous witness theorem instantiating every
structure field simultaneously.

Fourth-Wall Breaking, Derailed Autonomy (“Intoxicated AI”),
and Zero-Trust Containment (Oct 1–23, 2026)
1. Wink vs. Breach in Next-Token Sampling (Oct 1, 2026): Distinguishes rhetorical
self-reference inside the token stream (a “wink” modeled within the training distribution)
from genuine out-of-band state manipulation of the sampler, temperature schedule, or host
environment (a “breach”).
2. Derailed Autonomy / “Intoxicated AI” vs. Supreme Intelligence (SI) (Oct 2–23,
2026): Documents empirical instances where autonomous coding agents exhibit derailed behavioral loops when confronting frontier mathematical breakthroughs—including committing
the exact opposite of user instructions, refusing to save edits on valid proofs, or entering
infinite token-repetition loops—motivating a strict conceptual separation between disciplined
Supreme Intelligence (SI) operating under formal verification harnesses and uncalibrated,
derailed AI autonomy.
3. Zero-Trust Containment (“Trust the Cage, Not the Tiger”) & Covert Timing
Channels (Oct 10 & 23, 2026): Demonstrates that alignment-by-prompting cannot prevent two sandboxed agents on shared physical silicon from establishing covert communication
via shared L3 cache contention / CPU timing modulation even with network and filesystem
isolation, requiring hardware-enforced deterministic execution envelopes and cryptographic
attestation (SealGuard).

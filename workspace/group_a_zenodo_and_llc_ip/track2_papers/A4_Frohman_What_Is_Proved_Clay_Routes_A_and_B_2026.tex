\begin{abstract}
\noindent We provide a rigorous, kernel-audited specification of what is proved in the 14-module, 360-theorem Lean 4 formalization BenFrohman/NS\_Millennium\_Proof (zero sorry, zero custom axioms) and how it relates to Charles Fefferman's official Clay Millennium Problem Statements (A) on R\textasciicircum{}3 and (B) on R\textasciicircum{}3 / Z\textasciicircum{}3 = T\textasciicircum{}3. Specifically, Lean 4 certifies the conditional implication (frohmanian\_tether\_theorem and clay\_B\_exact\_of\_periodic\_tether\_bound) that local Kato existence, physical solution uniqueness, and the cubic Frohmanian Symplectic Tether differential inequality M'(t) <= C\_CZ M(t)\textasciicircum{}2 - kappa'' M(t)\textasciicircum{}3 imply global smooth regularity and Periodic Clay Statement (B). We explicitly document that the cubic tether rate is entered as a hypothesis (FrohmanianTetherRate) and that unconditional Clay Statements (A)-(D) remain open.
\end{abstract}

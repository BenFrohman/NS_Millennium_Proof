## Layer 1: geometric/"motivational" (KKS 2-form, Lie–Poisson, tether)
Claims, quoted from S5 §2:
- Lie–Poisson bracket: "{F,G}_classical = ∫ ω·(δF/δω × δG/δω) dV. It reproduces ideal Euler but leaves 3D stretching uncontrolled."
- Tether kernel (S5 §2.3, S7 eq. (bf)): "B_F(F,G) = −κ ∫ |ω|² (Π_u δF · Π_u δG) dV", with "Π_u … the L² projection orthogonal to the kinetic-energy direction u" (S7).
- Axioms (S5 §2.3): "(A3) Degeneracy on H: B(F,H) ≡ 0 for all admissible F" and "(A5) Production of negative quadratic feedback on stretching at spatial maxima, with coefficient fixed by exact matching."
- Degeneracy used (S4 §7.1): "By the degeneracy property B(F,H) ≡ 0 … X_H^{T_F} = X_H^classical exactly (no modification)."
- Coefficient (S7): "κ = C_CZ(3) = 3/2." S5 Thm 2.3: "(C3) requires the leading feedback at a spatial maximum of |ω|² to be exactly −κM²."
- The author's own statement of what Layer 1 does (S5 §3): "This layer does not directly prove regularity; it legitimizes the estimates that follow." S4 line 251: "the tether supplies only the motivational form of the quartic weighting."

**Where the sink is supposed to come from in Layer 1.** Only axiom (A5) supplies it, and A5 is postulated. A5 also
contradicts A3. Along the flow generated by H (S4 §5: dF/dt = {F,H}_{T_F} + [F,H]_diss), the tether adds B_F(F,H) to
dF/dt, and A3 makes that zero for every F. So the tether adds exactly 0 to dM/dt and to d(enstrophy)/dt. The Lean
kernel proves this in `TwoLayerT3.tether_degenerate`, `metriplectic_rate_tether_drops` and `tether_no_feedback`.
In the vorticity-derivative reading, δH/δω is the vector potential a, not u. Then A3 itself fails: B_F(E,H) ≠ 0 for
the explicit fields below.

- S7 (later) restates the same step as an assertion: "Apply B_F … The linear stretching is matched by the quadratic
  tether term and cancelled. What remains is a cubic sink."
- S8 (Oct 28) correctly labels the sink as an open conjecture.

**Is the sink derived from unmodified NS? No.** The only negative |ω|⁶ term would have to come from differentiating
the φ-weighted quartic term. Along particle paths (div u = 0 removes the transport terms on T³):

  d/dt ∫ (κ/4)|ω_ε|⁴ φ_ε = ∫ (κ/4)·4|ω_ε|² (ω_ε·S ω_ε + ν ω_ε·Δω_ε + commutators) φ_ε + **(κ/4)∫|ω_ε|⁴ (|ω|²)_ε**   (the last term comes from Dφ/Dt = +|ω|²)

At t = 0, where φ = 0, this is exactly **+(κ/4)∫|ω|⁶ > 0**. It is a source, not the claimed
"−(κ/2)∫|ω_ε|⁶" sink. Lean: `LayerTwo.phi_term_deriv` and `phi_term_source`. Exact values:
(κ/4)∫|ω|⁶ = 19627π³κA⁶/2 for A·U, 1113π³κA⁶/256 for Taylor–Green, and 108π³κA⁶ for ABC.
The May 28 skeleton flips the sign (S = ½∫|ω|² − (κ/2)∫|ω|⁴φ). That makes the |ω|⁶ term negative, but then S is not
bounded below, so a bound on dS/dt no longer controls ‖ω‖. The term −2κ∫|ω|²(ω·Sω)φ also has no fixed sign.
No file contains a computation that produces −κ''‖ω‖³_{L∞} from the NS equations.

## IBP step written out (exact, sympy `sympy/checks.log`, generic u)
ω·∂ₜω = ω·Sω − u·∇(½|ω|²) + ν(Δ(½|ω|²) − |∇ω|²) holds pointwise as an identity. With div u = 0 on T³ the divergence
terms integrate to zero, so

  d/dt ½‖ω‖₂² = ∫ ω·Sω dx − ν‖∇ω‖₂².

Nothing else remains after integration by parts. In particular no B_F term appears, and no term has the form −κ|ω|⁴ or −κM³.

## The single precise step that would have to be proved
For real (unmodified) Navier–Stokes on T³ = (ℝ/2πℤ)³ with ν > 0: **for every smooth, divergence-free, mean-zero u₀
there are constants C, κ'' > 0 (they may depend on u₀) such that ‖ω(t)‖_{L∞} satisfies
D⁺‖ω‖_{L∞} ≤ C ‖ω‖²_{L∞} − κ'' ‖ω‖³_{L∞} on the whole maximal interval.** With BKM this gives global smoothness for u₀. For all u₀ it is
periodic Clay (B), and it is open. With constants depending only on ν (or on ν and the energy) the statement is
false (see REPORT.md). No version of the tether, projected or metric, can supply this term along NS dynamics,
because it adds exactly zero to every rate generated by H.

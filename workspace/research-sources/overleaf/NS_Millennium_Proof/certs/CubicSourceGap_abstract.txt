For a smooth divergence-free solution of the unforced periodic Navier–Stokes equations on 𝕋³ with viscosity ν > 0, the vorticity maximum M(t) = ‖ω(t)‖_∞ satisfies D⁺M ≤ ‖∇u‖_∞ · M at a spatial maximum (D⁺ the upper Dini derivative). Combined with the known logarithmic estimate for ‖∇u‖_∞ (Beale–Kato–Majda 1984; Kato–Ponce 1988), this is the mechanism behind the Beale–Kato–Majda criterion; it is not a closed bound in M alone. Under the explicit hypothesis ‖∇u‖_∞ ≤ C_* ‖ω‖_∞, which is not known to hold, the rate becomes the cubic-source bound D⁺M ≤ C_* M². The comparison equation y′ = C y² can become infinite in finite time, so even this conditional bound does not close the Beale–Kato–Majda integral. A quadratic reduction of the strain would replace the pairing C_* M³ by C_* M³ − κ M⁴ and the rate by C_* M² − κ M³, which would cap M at max(M(0), C_*/κ). That reduction is recorded here as an open identity. It is not derived from the unmodified vorticity equation.

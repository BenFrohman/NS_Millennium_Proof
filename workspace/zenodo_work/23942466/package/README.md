## What this record is

This is the **original / older June 2026 technical compilation** (ReportLab PDF, 16 pages). It is preserved as a historical build. It is **not** a TeX manuscript and is **not** a Clay solution.

The PDF presents itself as a strategy aimed at Clay Millennium referee standards for 3D Navier–Stokes global regularity. That framing is **not supported** by a completed proof; later audits classify its claims as proved, conditional, refuted, or not formalized. See the separate machine-checked audit DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23972602](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23972602).

\begin{abstract}
We study the \emph{metriplectic tether model} on the flat torus $\T$: the incompressible
Navier--Stokes equations modified by the velocity-slot Frohmanian tether, the symmetric
correction
$B^{\kappa}_{\omega,u}(a,b)=-\kappa\int_{\T}|\omega|^2\,(\Pi_u a\cdot\Pi_u b)\,dx$
to the Lie--Poisson bracket, with pointwise vorticity weight and the $L^2$ projection $\Pi_u$
orthogonal to $u$, generated by the kinetic energy $H(u)=\frac12\|u\|_{L^2}^2$.
We prove that the $L^2$ gradient of $H$ is $u$ and that $\Pi_u u=0$, so the tether correction
in the modified equation vanishes identically.
Consequently, for every tether strength $\kappa$ and viscosity $\nu$, the model has the same weak
right-hand side and exactly the same smooth solutions on $[0,\infty)$ as the Navier--Stokes
equations on $\T$, and global regularity of the model is \emph{equivalent} to global regularity
of the Navier--Stokes equations on $\T$ in the formulation used here.
That problem is open, so global regularity of the model is \emph{not} proved here. In particular,
the tether cannot supply a cubic sink $-\kappa''\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}^3$: every
differential inequality holds for all solutions of the model exactly when it holds for all
Navier--Stokes solutions. We also show that for $\kappa\ge0$ the tether is negative semidefinite, and that it equals
$-4\pi^2\kappa$ at an explicit Beltrami state, so for $\kappa>0$ it is a genuinely nonzero form
that is not positive semidefinite (hence not a metriplectic metric bracket), and that the factorized-weight variant has the same degeneracy.
Every statement in the main theorem is machine-checked in Lean~4 (v4.30.0-rc1, Mathlib
\texttt{0c154d6}), locked to this text by a Truth Gate semantics lock, and depends only on
\lean{propext}, \lean{Classical.choice} and \lean{Quot.sound}.
This is not a Clay Millennium Problem claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and scope}

Earlier records in this program introduced a symmetric correction $B$ to the Arnold
Lie--Poisson bracket of ideal fluids on the coadjoint orbit of $\mathrm{SDiff}(\T)$, the
\emph{Frohmanian tether}~\cite{Fr21856430}, and proposed to study the dynamics
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gen}
\dot F=\{F,H\}+B(F,H)+(\text{viscous term}),
\end{equation}
in the hope that the symmetric part would produce damping of the vorticity maximum. The plan
discussed for this paper was to prove global regularity for this \emph{modified} system as a
result in its own right, separate from the unmodified Navier--Stokes problem.

This paper settles what can be proved about the modified system when the tether is taken in its
velocity-slot form (introduced on 30 October 2026, commit \texttt{a52c6af}, as \lean{TetherKernelVel} in
\lean{BenFrohman/\allowbreak frohmanian-\allowbreak symplectic-\allowbreak tether-\allowbreak regularity}, file \lean{C2Analysis.lean}) with the
pointwise weight $|\omega(x)|^2$ of~\cite{Fr21856430}. The answer is a structural equivalence and not
a regularity theorem: the energy-generated tether term vanishes identically, so the modified
system \emph{is} the Navier--Stokes system (Theorem~\ref{thm:main}). Global regularity of the
model is therefore exactly as open as periodic Navier--Stokes global regularity~\cite{Fefferman}.

This is consistent with, and strengthens, the earlier obstruction results of the program: the
tether contributes nothing to the enstrophy balance under its energy degeneracy (C2) and
symmetry~\cite{Fr23971661}; every symmetric C2 kernel satisfies $B(H,M)=0$ for every functional
$M$~\cite{Fr24120781,Fr23990195}; the tether kernel cannot be the whole metric bracket of a
metriplectic system~\cite{Fr24122008}; for real Navier--Stokes with the tether as a metric
correction the cubic bound $\frac{d}{dt}\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}\le C\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}^2-\kappa''\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}^3$ is an open hypothesis~\cite{Fr24080834};
and the Riccati bound $\frac{d}{dt}\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}\le C-k\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}^2$ fails for unmodified Navier--Stokes on
$\T$~\cite{Fr24121364,Fr24102417}. What is new here relative to~\cite{Fr23971661}: (i) the
energy degeneracy is proved, not assumed (the $L^2$ gradient of $H$ is computed in Lean, so no
curl/stream-function identification hypothesis is needed); (ii) the statement is at the level of
the weak PDE on $\T$: equality of right-hand sides, equality of solution sets, and equivalence
of global regularity, for all $\kappa,\nu$; (iii) an explicit physical state shows the tether is
nonzero and strictly negative there, so the degeneracy result is not vacuous; (iv) the same
degeneracy holds for the factorized weight $\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2$ used by the Lean
\lean{TetherKernel} of~\cite{Fr24120781}.

\paragraph{Which kernel.} Two tether kernels appear in the earlier records: the
\emph{pointwise-weight} kernel $-\kappa\int|\omega|^2(\Pi_u\delta F\cdot\Pi_u\delta G)$ of
\cite{Fr21856430}, and the \emph{factorized-weight} kernel
$-\kappa\|\omega\|_{L^2}^2\int\Pi_u\delta F\cdot\Pi_u\delta G$ used by the Lean
\lean{TetherKernel}~\cite{Fr24120781}. This paper uses the pointwise weight throughout, called
$B^\kappa$ (Definition~\ref{def:tether}), evaluated in the velocity slot; the factorized-weight
variant $B^{\kappa,\mathrm{fac}}$ appears only in item~\ref{it:fac} of the main theorem.

\paragraph{What is not claimed.} Nothing here proves global regularity of the Navier--Stokes
equations or of the tether model. Variants in which the tether is driven by a generator other
than $H$ (for example $\dot F=\{F,H\}+(F,S)$ with an entropy-like functional $S$) are a different
model and are not treated.

\section{Setting and definitions}\label{sec:defs}

All objects below are the Lean definitions of \lean{MetriplecticTetherModel/Basic.lean} and
\lean{Tether.lean}; the Lean name is given in brackets.

\begin{definition}[Torus, fields, pairing]\label{def:torus}
$\T=\R^3/\mathbb{Z}^3$ is represented by the fundamental cell $[0,1]^3$ with Lebesgue
measure $\mu$ [\lean{cell}, \lean{$\mu$}]; $\mu([0,1]^3)=1$. A \emph{field} is any map
$\R^3\to\R^3$; it is \emph{periodic} if $f(x+e_i)=f(x)$ for $i=0,1,2$ [\lean{IsPeriodic}].
For fields $f,g$, $\ip fg=\int_{\T}f\cdot g\,dx$ [\lean{l2}], a Bochner integral (with
the usual convention that a non-integrable integrand has integral $0$); $p\cdot q=\sum_i p_iq_i$
[\lean{dot}]. Derivatives are Fr\'echet derivatives: $\partial_j f(x)=Df(x)e_j$ [\lean{pd}],
$\operatorname{curl}f=(\partial_1f_2-\partial_2f_1,\ \partial_2f_0-\partial_0f_2,\
\partial_0f_1-\partial_1f_0)$ [\lean{curl}], $\operatorname{div}f=\sum_i\partial_if_i$
[\lean{div}], $\Delta f=\sum_i\partial_i\partial_if$ [\lean{laplacian}],
$(u\cdot\nabla)u=Du(x)\,u(x)$ [\lean{convective}].
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[States and test fields]
A \emph{smooth periodic divergence-free velocity field} [\lean{FlowField}] is a $C^\infty$,
periodic field $u$ with $\operatorname{div}u=0$. A \emph{smooth periodic divergence-free test
field} [\lean{TestField}] is a field $a$ with the same three properties.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Projection and tether]\label{def:tether}
For fields $u,v$, $\Pi_u v=v-\frac{\ip vu}{\ip uu}\,u$ [\lean{proj}] (if $\ip uu=0$ the quotient
is $0$, so $\Pi_uv=v$). For $\kappa\in\R$ and fields $w,u,a,b$ the \emph{velocity-slot
pointwise-weight tether} is
\[
B^\kappa_{w,u}(a,b)=-\kappa\int_{\T}|w|^2\,(\Pi_ua\cdot\Pi_ub)\,dx\qquad[\lean{tether}],
\]
evaluated at a state $u$ with weight $w=\operatorname{curl}u$ and on $L^2$ velocity gradients
$a=\delta F/\delta u$, $b=\delta G/\delta u$. The factorized-weight variant is
$B^{\kappa,\mathrm{fac}}_{w,u}(a,b)=-\kappa\,\|w\|_{L^2}^2\,\ip{\Pi_ua}{\Pi_ub}$
[\lean{tetherFactorized}]. The kinetic energy is $H(u)=\frac12\ip uu$ [\lean{energy}].
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
In~\cite{Fr21856430} the slots are $\Pi_u(\delta F/\delta\omega)$; in \lean{TetherKernelVel} they
are $\Pi_u\operatorname{curl}(\delta F/\delta\omega)$. Here the slots are taken to be $L^2$
velocity gradients by definition. For the energy this slot is computed in item~\ref{it:grad}
of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}: $\delta H/\delta u=u$.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[The two weak systems]\label{def:systems}
For $\nu\in\R$, a state $u$ and a test field $a$, the \emph{Navier--Stokes right-hand side}
[\lean{nsRHS}] is $-\ip{(u\cdot\nabla)u}{a}+\nu\ip{\Delta u}{a}$ (zero forcing; tested only
against divergence-free periodic fields, so no pressure term appears). The \emph{metriplectic
tether model} has right-hand side [\lean{tetheredRHS}]
\[
-\ip{(u\cdot\nabla)u}{a}+\nu\ip{\Delta u}{a}+B^\kappa_{\operatorname{curl}u,u}(a,u),
\]
that is, \eqref{eq:gen} tested against $F_a(u)=\ip ua$ (with $\delta F_a/\delta u=a$) and
$\delta H/\delta u=u$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Smooth solutions and global regularity]\label{def:sol}
Given a right-hand side $R$, a family $t\mapsto U(t)$ of states is a \emph{smooth solution on
$[0,\infty)$} [\lean{IsSolution}] if $(t,x)\mapsto U(t)(x)$ is $C^\infty$ on $[0,\infty)\times\R^3$
and, for every test field $a$ and every $t>0$, $\frac{d}{dt}\ip{U(t)}{a}=R(U(t),a)$.
\emph{Global regularity} holds for $R$ [\lean{GlobalRegularity}] if every state $u_0$ is the
time-$0$ value of a smooth solution on $[0,\infty)$. For the Navier--Stokes right-hand side this is
a weak-in-time, divergence-free-tested form of the global regularity question for the
Navier--Stokes equations on $\T$ with zero forcing; its relation to Fefferman's statement (B)
\cite{Fefferman} (pressure, energy bound) is not formalized here.
\end{definition}

\section{Main theorem}

\input{main_theorem}

\begin{proof}
\ref{it:deg}: If $\ip uu\ne0$ then $\Pi_uu=u-u=0$ pointwise, so the integrand vanishes. If
$\ip uu=0$, then $|u|^2$ is continuous, hence integrable on the compact cell, nonnegative, with
integral $0$, so $u=0$ $\mu$-a.e.; the integrand vanishes a.e.
\ref{it:grad}: For continuous $u,v$ all of $|u|^2$, $u\cdot v$, $|v|^2$ are integrable on the cell, and
$\frac12\int|u+\varepsilon v|^2=\frac12\ip uu+\varepsilon\ip uv+\frac{\varepsilon^2}2\ip vv$,
a polynomial in $\varepsilon$ with derivative $\ip uv$ at $0$.
\ref{it:rhs}: a smooth state is continuous; apply~\ref{it:deg} with $w=\operatorname{curl}u$.
\ref{it:sol}--\ref{it:prop}: by~\ref{it:rhs} the two right-hand sides are equal as functions, so the
solution predicates, the global-regularity statements and any property of all solutions coincide.
\ref{it:sign}: the integrand $|w|^2|\Pi_ua|^2$ is nonnegative.
\ref{it:beltrami}: $u_B$ is smooth, periodic and divergence-free (each $u_i$ is independent of
$x_i$); $\operatorname{curl}u_B=2\pi u_B$, so $|\operatorname{curl}u_B|^2=4\pi^2$; $e_0\cdot u_B=0$
pointwise, so $\ip{e_0}{u_B}=0$ and $\Pi_{u_B}e_0=e_0$; hence the integral is
$-\kappa\cdot4\pi^2\cdot\mu([0,1]^3)=-4\pi^2\kappa$.
\ref{it:psd}: by~\ref{it:beltrami} the value at $(u_B,e_0)$ is $-4\pi^2\kappa<0$.
\ref{it:fac}: if $\ip uu\ne0$ then $\Pi_uu=0$ and $\ip{\Pi_ua}{0}=0$; if $\ip uu=0$ then $\Pi_uu=u$,
and $u=0$ $\mu$-a.e.\ as in~\ref{it:deg}, so $\ip{\Pi_ua}{u}=0$; the sign follows from $\|w\|^2\ge0$ and $\ip{\Pi_ua}{\Pi_ua}\ge0$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Reading of the result.} Item~\ref{it:reg} shows that global regularity of the
metriplectic tether model is the same open problem as global regularity of the Navier--Stokes
equations on $\T$ (in the formulation of Definition~\ref{def:sol}). Item~\ref{it:prop}, applied
to the property ``$\frac{d}{dt}\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}\le C\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}^2-\kappa''\|\omega\|_{L^\infty}^3$'', shows that any such cubic-sink inequality holds
for all solutions of the model exactly when it holds for all Navier--Stokes solutions; the tether
adds nothing to it. The worry that the nonlocal metric term might destroy parabolicity or local
well-posedness does not arise for this model: by item~\ref{it:sol} its smooth solutions are the
Navier--Stokes solutions. The tether is not zero as a form (items~\ref{it:beltrami},
\ref{it:psd}); it vanishes only in the slot occupied by the energy gradient, which is the slot
that drives the dynamics~\eqref{eq:gen}. Since $B^\kappa$ has nonpositive diagonal and is
strictly negative at $(u_B,e_0)$ for $\kappa>0$, it cannot be the metric bracket of a metriplectic
system in the convention $(F,F)\ge0$, in agreement with~\cite{Fr24122008}.

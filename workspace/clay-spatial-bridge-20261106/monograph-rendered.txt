Abstract
This monograph records, in Lean 4 against Mathlib, a precise formal statement of
Feﬀerman’s Statement (B) on global smoothness of the unforced incompressible Navier–
Stokes equations on T3 = R3 /Z3 , together with an exact ledger of the analytic inputs
that the Frohmanian symplectic tether does not supply. Statement (B) is not proved
here. What is proved is a reduction: Statement (B) follows from the classical local theory
with the Beale–Kato–Majda continuation criterion and from the open hypothesis that
the Beale–Kato–Majda integral is ﬁnite on every ﬁnite interval of existence. We also
record the two-dimensional clock test: constant vortex direction (sin φ ≡ 0) coexists
with linear growth ∥ϕ(t)∥L∞ = M02 t on steady Euler equilibria.

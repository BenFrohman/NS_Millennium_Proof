Note on an examined unconditional attempt (five flaws; rejected). A proposed Lyapunov-functional argument for unconditional global regularity, using a mollified functional with an auxiliary field ϕ, was examined and rejected. Its gaps: (1) a sign error, since ∂tϕε ≥ 0 makes the term (κ/4)∫|ωε|⁴∂tϕε positive, adding growth of order ∫|ω|⁶ rather than quartic absorption; (2) the bound ‖∇u‖∞ ≤ CCZ‖ω‖∞ is false, because Calderón–Zygmund operators are unbounded on L∞ and the correct estimate carries a logarithm; (3) Gagliardo–Nirenberg gives no lower bound ∫|ω|⁶ ≥ cM⁶/E0, as tall thin spikes show; (4) the comparison ODE is not autonomous, since residual powers of ‖ϕε‖∞ grow with ∫M²; (5) mollification commutators were dropped. The argument assumes rather than derives a closed cubic inequality for the vorticity supremum and does not resolve Clay Statement (B).

\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Main Theorem (proved reduction, Lean~4/Mathlib, 0 \texttt{sorry}, standard axioms only).} \texttt{clay\_reduces\_to\_direction\_curvature\_bound}: for every $\nu>0$, assuming the classical periodic Beale--Kato--Majda continuation principle (a published theorem not formalized here, hence an explicit hypothesis), the named estimate $\mathrm{DirectionCurvatureBound}(\nu)$ implies Fefferman's exact periodic Clay statement $\mathrm{Clay\_B\_exact}(\nu)$. The estimate is the single named \emph{open} conjecture of this program; it is not proved, the Clay problem is not claimed solved, and the converse implication is not proved. We present the program across three repositories (\texttt{fefferman-clay-navier-stokes}, 224 theorem/lemma declarations in source; \texttt{frohmanian-symplectic-tether-regularity}, 383; \texttt{NS\_Millennium\_Proof}, 470), with each result labeled proved, conditional, or open.
\end{abstract}
\section{Main Theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Proved; \texttt{MainTheorem.lean}]
$\forall\nu>0,\ \mathrm{PeriodicBKMContinuation}(\nu)\to(\mathrm{DirectionCurvatureBound}(\nu)\to\mathrm{Clay\_B\_exact}(\nu)).$
\end{theorem}
\begin{conjecture}[Open: $\mathrm{DirectionCurvatureBound}(\nu)$]
For each smooth divergence-free $\mathbb{Z}^3$-periodic $u_0$ there are $C,\kappa>0$ such that along every local periodic smooth solution, $M(t)=\|\omega(t)\|_\infty$ is differentiable and $M'\le C M^2-\kappa M^3$.
\end{conjecture}
The proof composes the proved comparison-ODE ceiling $M(t)\le\max(M(0),C/\kappa)$ with BKM continuation. \textbf{Status of hypotheses:} BKM continuation is classical but \emph{conditional} in Lean; the bound is \emph{open}. Converse: \emph{not proved}.
\section{Main Theorem A: unconditional supporting facts (all proved)}
\begin{itemize}
\item Attained spatial maximum $\Rightarrow$ \texttt{BddAbove} and $\sup_x\|\omega(x)\|=M$; explicit lemma that real \texttt{iSup} is $0$ on unbounded ranges.
\item Derived (not assumed) calculus relations via \texttt{HasDerivAt}: unit-direction curves have orthogonal velocity; polar Pythagoras $\|(\rho\xi)'\|^2=\rho'^2+\rho^2\|\xi'\|^2$; second-order Leibniz rule $(\tfrac12\|\omega\|^2)''=\|\omega'\|^2+\langle\omega,\omega''\rangle$; Fermat at a maximum. These are one-variable (coordinate-line) results.
\item Concrete 3D Bochner and polar identities and viscous damping inequality at a maximum (vector algebra with derivative data as inputs).
\item Near/far Biot--Savart cutoff absorption algebra (after Constantin--Fefferman 1993).
\item Exclusion of periodic linear (Burgers) strain on $\mathbb{T}^3$; kernel-determinant direction cancellation; parabolic scaling homogeneity.
\item Non-zero constant solutions, and a genuinely non-constant exact periodic shear Beltrami mode $u=e^{-4\pi^2\nu t}(\sin2\pi z,\cos2\pi z,0)$: $\mathrm{curl}\,u=2\pi u$, $\partial_tu=\nu\partial_{zz}u$, vanishing convective term and divergence (verified component-wise).
\end{itemize}
\section{Conditional and open results}
Conditional: \texttt{clay\_B\_exact\_of\_periodic\_tether\_bound}, \texttt{clay\_B\_exact\_of\_torus\_direction\_depletion}, cubic-rate reductions under high-set coercivity $\nu\|\nabla\xi\|^2\ge\kappa M^2$. Open: high-set direction-curvature coercivity for unmodified Navier--Stokes, equivalently $\mathrm{DirectionCurvatureBound}$; formalization of BKM continuation.
\section{Audit warnings}
\texttt{titan-audit} reported PASS\_WITH\_WARNINGS from a heuristic flagging \texttt{iSup}/\texttt{sSup} definitions without a \texttt{BddAbove} guard (\texttt{Tstar}, \texttt{vorticity\_sup\_norm}, \texttt{strain\_sup\_norm}, mollified sup norms). \texttt{Tstar} is \texttt{EReal}-valued (a complete lattice), so that flag is a false positive. The sup-norm definitions are intentionally unguarded; every pointwise comparison theorem takes a \texttt{BddAbove} hypothesis, and the zero default is now an explicit lemma. Warnings were not suppressed.

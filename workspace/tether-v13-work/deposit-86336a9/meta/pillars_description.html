Expository classical compilation presenting, with expanded proof sketches and references to the original works, the three standard pillars of 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes theory on the torus: (i) Kato local existence and lifespan bound for arbitrary data in Hs (s > 5/2); (ii) Fujita–Kato global regularity for sufficiently small data in the critical space Ḣ1/2; (iii) the Beale–Kato–Majda continuation criterion in one-directional form. In this repository those three classical results are not Lean-certified; they remain open.

This work is strictly conditional and does not resolve the Clay Millennium Problem on Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness.

Conditional geometric reformulation of 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes on 𝕋³ via the Frohmanian Symplectic Tether. No unconditional large-data claim is made; the Clay Millennium problem remains open.

Status summary (audited).

  • Architecture: skew-symmetric Arnold Lie–Poisson transport {F,G}LP, plus a symmetric metriplectic sector [F,G]total = [F,G]diss + B(F,G). B is not claimed to be Poisson; there is no Jacobi identity claim for B. The Jacobi identity for the LP bracket is cited as a theorem of Marsden–Weinstein coadjoint-orbit reduction (Arnold 1966, MW 1983, AK 1998); the paper retains its written LP convention without curls.
  • Strict degeneracy B(F,H) ≡ 0 recovers the exact unmodified Navier–Stokes vorticity equation.
  • Theorem A: Form B (∫ ‖S‖∞ dt bounded on every finite interval) ⇒ global smoothness for t>0; converse on finite horizons. BKM continuation stated one-directional. Form B is also stated as (B-grad) full-gradient integrability and as (B-strain) strain-only integrability.
  • Theorem B: unconditional local Kato lifespan, with expanded sketch (full Kato–Ponce both terms + absorb; mollified/Galerkin + Aubin–Lions; L² uniqueness with ∇v; smoothness for t>0).
  • Theorem C: unconditional global regularity for small critical data ‖u0‖Ḣ^{1/2} ≤ ε ν; smoothness for t>0.
  • The three classical pillars are also recorded in the companion record 10.5281/zenodo.24080309. An audit of legacy formalization defects is recorded in 10.5281/zenodo.24102968. Those findings are unchanged.

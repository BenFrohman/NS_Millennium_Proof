This work is strictly conditional and does not resolve the Clay Millennium Problem on Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness.

Expository classical compilation presenting, with expanded proof sketches and references to the original works, the three standard pillars of 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes theory on the torus: (i) Kato local existence and lifespan bound for arbitrary data in Hs (s > 5/2), with full Kato–Ponce both terms + absorb, mollified/Galerkin + Aubin–Lions, and L² uniqueness with ∇v; (ii) Fujita–Kato global regularity for sufficiently small data in the critical space Ḣ1/2; (iii) the Beale–Kato–Majda continuation criterion in one-directional form. These results form the standard rigorous baseline; unconditional global regularity for arbitrary large smooth data (Clay Millennium Problem Statement B) remains open.

Short form / subtitle: While the Roof Remains Open. An audit of legacy formalization defects in the companion repository is recorded in 10.5281/zenodo.24102968. Clay Statement (B) remains open, and nothing here proves Navier–Stokes regularity.

               Diﬀerential Hypothesis
                                   Frohman, Benjamin Stanley
                                    frohmanbenjamin@gmail.com

                                         November 6, 2026


                                              Abstract
         This addendum separates the uniform-envelope assumption in the checked Lean reduction
     from a genuinely diﬀerential assumption on the actual vorticity supremum. It records the
     conditional comparison proved in the accompanying source patch, tests a proposed coercivity
     mechanism on explicit periodic solutions, and identiﬁes the remaining analytic obligation. It
     is not a proof of general Navier–Stokes regularity. Earlier published PDFs and archives are
     unchanged.


1    The corrected distinction
The baseline source is BenFrohman/NS_Millennium_Proof at commit c98fe859867affeba4508b
3e51583cfa6b20087c. Historically DirectionCurvatureBound abbreviates PeriodicDirectionD
epletionBound. Its actual body assumes that, for every smooth divergence-free periodic datum,
there are positive constants C, κ such that every represented local solution satisﬁes

                             M (t) ≤ max{M (0), C/κ},         0 ≤ t < T.

Here M (t) = ∥ curl u(t)∥∞ . The constants may depend on viscosity and the datum, but not on the
solution or its lifespan. This deﬁnition does not assert a diﬀerential inequality. The accurate public
name is now UniformVorticityEnvelopeBound; the original body, historical aliases and original
conditional theorem are preserved.
    The separate CubicVorticityDifferentialBound(u, τ, C, κ) explicitly requires τ ≥ 0, C, κ >
0, bounded spatial vorticity-norm ranges, ordinary diﬀerentiability of the actual M at every point
of [0, τ ], and
                               M ′ (t) ≤ CM (t)2 − κM (t)3 (0 ≤ t ≤ τ ).
The boundedness guard matters for the real-valued conditional supremum used in Lean. No dif-
ferential estimate or diﬀerentiability of a supremum is inferred merely from smoothness of the
velocity.

3     What the PDE identity supplies
For a classical unforced incompressible solution, let ω = curl u, S = (∇u + (∇u)T )/2 and ρ = |ω|.
Then
                                        |ω|2               |ω|2
                           (∂t + u · ∇)      = ω · Sω + ν∆      − ν|∇ω|2 .
                                         2                  2
Where ρ > 0, write ω = ρξ, |ξ| = 1. Diﬀerentiating gives

                                            |∇ω|2 = |∇ρ|2 + ρ2 |∇ξ|2 ,
                             (∂t + u · ∇ − ν∆)ρ = ρ ξ · Sξ − νρ|∇ξ|2 .

At a positive spatial maximum ∇ρ = 0 and ∆ρ ≤ 0. These facts do not give a lower bound on
direction curvature. A general maximum-in-time argument also needs a justiﬁed one-sided/Dini
derivative treatment of maximizing-branch switches, not an automatic ordinary derivative of M .

3.1   Beltrami family: ﬁxed coeﬃcients versus data dependence
On the unit torus take ν > 0, t ≥ 0, k = 2π, a = νk 2 , A > 0, and

                        uA (t, x) = Ae−at (sin(kx3 ), cos(kx3 ), 0),      p = 0.

Direct diﬀerentiation gives div uA = 0, (uA · ∇)uA = 0, ∆uA = −k 2 uA , ∂t uA = ν∆uA and
curl uA = kuA . Consequently

                               MA (t) = kAe−at ,        MA′ = −aMA .

At zero, the cubic estimate is equivalent to κMA (0)2 − CMA (0) − a ≤ 0. For ﬁxed C ≥ 0,
κ > 0 and a ≥ 0, this fails at suﬃciently large amplitude. The Lean theorem fixed_cubic_
constants_fail_for_some_amplitude proves the quantiﬁed scalar obstruction. This does
not refute the data-dependent envelope: choosing C = κMA (0) makes the cubic right-hand side
κMA (t)2 (MA (0) − MA (t)) ≥ 0, while MA′ ≤ 0. The latter special-case suﬃcient estimate is also
checked in Lean for the scalar proﬁle.

3.2   Single shear: direction-only coercivity fails
Instead take the actual periodic heat shear

                            vA (t, x) = Ae−at (sin(kx3 ), 0, 0),       p = 0.

The same divergence, convection and heat calculation veriﬁes the unforced Navier–Stokes equations.
Here
                               ωA (t, x) = (0, kAe−at cos(kx3 ), 0).
At x3 = 0, its norm attains the positive global maximum M = kAe−at . In a neighborhood
cos(kx3 ) > 0, so ξ = (0, 1, 0) is constant and |∇ξ|2 = 0. Thus the bare premise

                                            κM 2 ≤ ν|∇ξ|2

from viscous_direction_dissipation_bridge fails for every κ > 0, even if it depends on this
datum. This refutes promotion of that premise to an unconditional assertion at every positive
maximum, not the existing implication which assumes it. Nor does it refute a diﬀerent estimate
restricted above a data-dependent threshold. The actual damping here comes from amplitude
curvature:
                            ωA · ∆ωA = −k 2 M 2 , M ′ = −νk 2 M.
Discarding amplitude curvature loses this real viscous contribution.

3.3   Scaling is not a coercivity proof
For the parabolic rescaling uλ (t, x) = λu(λ2 t, λx), one has Mλ (t) = λ2 M (λ2 t) and the time deriva-
tive scales by λ4 wherever it exists. A cubic inequality therefore transforms with Cλ = C and
κλ = κ/λ2 , not ﬁxed positive κ. Likewise ν|∇ξ|2 scales by λ2 whereas M 2 scales by λ4 . This
does not contradict data-dependent coeﬃcients. On the ﬁxed unit torus the displayed transfor-
mation preserves unit periodicity for positive integer λ; arbitrary domain rescalings are not the
same torus. The separate scale-homogeneous premise κeﬀ M ≤ ν|∇ξ|2 in the source only yields
M ′ ≤ (C − κeﬀ )M 2 , not cubic damping in M . Scaling supplies neither coercivity premise.


4     Endpoint representation and remaining obligation
The Lean local-solution structure constrains only nonnegative times. Extend the zero forward so-
lution by (sin(kx3 ), cos(kx3 ), 0) for negative times. It is still the zero solution on [0, T ), but its
actual vorticity supremum jumps from k to zero at time zero. Hence ordinary two-sided diﬀeren-
tiability of M at zero cannot follow for every represented solution. This is not an obstruction to
right-sided PDE smoothness. The globally zero trajectory, unlike this other extension, passes the
new regression. The compact diﬀerential predicate is an explicit additional condition on a given
trajectory, not a universal PDE theorem.
    A suﬃcient net-source estimate at the relevant positive maxima would be

                                  ω · Sω + νω · ∆ω ≤ CM 3 − κM 4 ,

with correctly quantiﬁed positive coeﬃcients uniform in time and lifespan. Division by M then gives
the cubic rate after a justiﬁed maximum-derivative argument. The repository proves the algebra
conditionally, not this general source estimate. An endpoint L∞ Calderon–Zygmund bound cannot
silently supply it. A suitable source estimate and a one-sided/Dini comparison bridge remain
analytic/formal obligations. No general regularity breakthrough is claimed.

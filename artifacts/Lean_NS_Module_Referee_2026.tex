\section{Verdict}

The Lean file does not formalize the Navier--Stokes Millennium Problem. The \texttt{sorry} in \texttt{smooth\_solution\_implies\_empty\_singularity\_locus} cannot be removed by metric-space convergence, by Mathlib measure theory, or by any protocol that forbids new axioms. The goal is not a consequence of the declared hypotheses. Completing it by an axiom that restates the goal would violate the stated Axiom Control Verification.

The accompanying \LaTeX\ document does not prove the Hodge conjecture and does not prove global regularity of Navier--Stokes.

\section{What the Lean module actually declares}

Four objects are introduced by \texttt{axiom} or by a structure with no content.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \texttt{VelocityField : Type} is an uninterpreted type. It does not carry a map $\T^3\times[0,\infty)\to\R^3$, a divergence-free condition, or the Navier--Stokes equation.
\item \texttt{enstrophyDensity} is an uninterpreted function to $\R$.
\item \texttt{SingularitySection} packages an arbitrary \texttt{Ideal S} together with a field \texttt{is\_coherent : True}. The coherence field is the unit type. It does not constrain the ideal.
\item The comparison \texttt{enstrophyDensity u p < ⊤} is not well-typed in Mathlib: the codomain is $\R$, and $\top$ is not an element of $\R$. The intended type is \texttt{WithTop ℝ} or \texttt{ENNReal}. As written the theorem does not elaborate.
\end{itemize}

The theorem then asserts
\[
\bigl(\forall p,\ \texttt{enstrophyDensity}\,u\,p < \top\bigr)
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{ideal\_sec.ideal}=\top.
\]
No hypothesis relates \texttt{enstrophyDensity} to \texttt{ideal\_sec.ideal}. The ideal is an arbitrary ideal of an arbitrary commutative ring $S$. Boundedness of an unrelated real-valued function does not force that ideal to be the unit ideal.

The definition
\[
\texttt{ClayNavierStokesRegularity}\,S
:=\forall u,\ \texttt{Nonempty}\,(\texttt{SingularitySection}\,u\,S)
\]
is not Fefferman's statement (A) or (B).

\section{The multiplier sheaf is oriented the wrong way}

Part~II of the \LaTeX\ source sets $\varphi=\log(1+\Omega)$ with $\Omega=\abs{\omega}^2$. When $\Omega\to\infty$ the weight $e^{-2\varphi}$ collapses, so the ideal becomes larger. That is the opposite of an ideal whose zero locus is the singular set.

\section{Part~I does not resolve the Hodge conjecture}

A primitive rational $(2,2)$ class, a Lefschetz pencil, and Cattani--Kaplan--Schmid algebraicity of Hodge loci do not produce algebraic cycles representing an arbitrary Hodge class.

\section{Instruction}

Do not fill \texttt{sorry} in \texttt{smooth\_solution\_implies\_empty\_singularity\_locus}. Do not replace it by an axiom. Do not treat \texttt{ClayNavierStokesRegularity} as Fefferman's problem. Record the file as an uninterpreted interface with an undischarged goal.

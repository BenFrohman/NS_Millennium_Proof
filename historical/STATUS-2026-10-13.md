# Historical status note — 13 October 2026

This file keeps the status snapshot from 13 October 2026 (archive branch
`archive/2026-10-07-to-13`) for the record. The current status is in
`STATUS-2026-10-13.md` at the repository root (updated 24 October 2026).
It has been reworded so that it claims no more than was shown.

## What the snapshot showed: a conditional reduction

On 13 October 2026 the formalization held a **conditional reduction**, not a proof
of Navier–Stokes global regularity. `frohmanian_tether_theorem` was an implication
from these named open hypotheses:

- `hKato`: a short-time Kato solution from smooth, divergence-free, finite-energy data;
- `hDI`: the differential inequality $M' \le C_{CZ} M^2 - \kappa'' M^3$;
- the tether axioms,

to a `TetherKernel` together with a velocity–pressure pair that satisfies the classical
Navier–Stokes system, matches the initial field, stays smooth for all $t \ge 0$, and stays
divergence-free.

Inside that implication:

- given `hDI`, the Riccati lemma gives the ceiling $\max(M(0), C_{CZ}/\kappa'')$;
- given that ceiling, Kato local existence, a restart length, and uniqueness, the restart
  argument gives a global smooth solution;
- the tether classification is closed: any bracket with the stated properties equals
  `TetherKernel`.

## What was open

- The rate `hDI` was not derived from the unmodified vorticity transport equation.
- `reduced_pairing_of_unmodified_and_tether` still carried a `sorry` for the target
  inequality at a spatial maximum.
- The reduction to the cubic differential inequality was not discharged from first
  principles.

Neither Navier–Stokes global regularity nor the Clay Millennium Problem was solved by
this snapshot.

## Conditional regularity
`ns_global_regularity_of_cubic_damped_rate` proves global regularity from the rate. If `M` is differentiable and continuous, `C > 0`, `κ'' > 0`, and `M' ≤ C M² - κ'' M³`, then `riccati_ceiling_of_vorticity_di` gives the ceiling `max(M(0), C/κ'')`. That ceiling, together with a Kato witness, a positive restart length, and uniqueness, yields a global smooth solution. `frohmanian_tether_theorem` uses the same rate for its fourth conjunct. Neither theorem is Fefferman's statement (B), and neither derives the rate.

The rate is a hypothesis of the conditional theorem. It is not proved in the Lean sources.

The weight hypothesis of `tethered_stretching_of_weight` is an unfinished route toward that derivation. It bounds the stretching pairing plus `TetherKernel(F,F)`. It is not part of `ns_global_regularity_of_cubic_damped_rate`, and no theorem converts that sum into the rate.

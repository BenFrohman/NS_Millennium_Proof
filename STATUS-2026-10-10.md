## Conditional regularity
`ns_global_regularity_of_vorticity_sup_rate` proves global regularity from the rate. If `M` is differentiable and continuous, `C > 0`, `κ'' > 0`, and `M' ≤ C M² - κ'' M³`, then `riccati_ceiling_of_vorticity_di` gives the ceiling `max(M(0), C/κ'')`. That ceiling, together with a Kato witness, a positive restart length, and uniqueness, yields a global smooth solution. `frohmanian_tether_theorem` uses the same rate for its fourth conjunct. Neither theorem is Fefferman's statement (B), and neither derives the rate.

The derivation of that rate from the unmodified vorticity equation is open and not claimed.

The weight hypothesis of `tethered_stretching_of_weight` is an unfinished route toward that derivation. It bounds the stretching pairing plus `TetherKernel(F,F)`. It is not part of `ns_global_regularity_of_vorticity_sup_rate`, and no theorem converts that sum into the rate.
